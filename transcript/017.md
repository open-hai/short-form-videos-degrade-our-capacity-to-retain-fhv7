The repository runs end to end from a clean clone. Here's what the audit found.

## What was done

**Paper:** Chiossi, Haliburton, Ou, Butz & Schmidt, *Short-Form Videos Degrade Our Capacity to Retain Intentions* (CHI '23, doi:10.1145/3544548.3580778) — a between-subjects study (N=60) where a 10-minute TikTok/Twitter/YouTube/Rest interruption sits between two blocks of a dual lexical-decision + prospective-memory task.

**Artifacts found (all fetched, all recorded in `SOURCES.md`):** the authors' GitHub repo with trial-level CC-0 data, an OSF project carrying two files GitHub does not have (the questionnaires and the YouTube playlist), and — after searching OSF registrations, OSF registries, Zenodo, GitHub org-wide, arXiv ancillary files and the ACM DL — **no preregistration** and **no experiment program**, despite Section 7 claiming the "experimental setup" is on GitHub.

**Inner loop implemented from scratch in Python** (`src/`, 12 modules): accuracy derivation, three accuracy LMMs, trial-level RT and binomial models, PyDDM fitting, the Shapiro-gated ART/parametric ANOVA of Table 1, post-hoc contrasts, JZS Bayes factors, Figures 3–6. It compares itself against 100 quantities quoted from the paper, each tagged with its section/table/figure.

## Verdict: partial — 9 reproduced, 6 partial, 1 blocked of 16 inner-loop components

The headline claim reproduces exactly: every β, CI and t of the PM accuracy model comes back at printed precision, 22 of 24 Table 1 F values match to three decimals, the questionnaire ANOVAs match, and two of three Bayes factors match to four digits.

Four findings an author would want:

- **The DDM is scale-unidentified.** σ is left free and the optimiser unseeded, so (μ, σ, B) are pinned only as a ray. Refits give bound r = 0.17 against the published values while μ/σ and B/σ come back at r > 0.99. Table 1's PM conclusions survive a refit at the authors' own `dt`; the LD null does not.
- **"No significant results" for reaction times is contradicted by the authors' own supplement** — and independently here (LD TikTok×post p < .001, PM YouTube×post p = .007).
- **The data hold 14 PM cue trials per block, not the stated 16**, and stimuli run 4–8 letters, not 6–8.
- **The LD model's R² is 0.99, not the 0.67 printed** (0.67 is the PM model's value).

The outer loop — recruitment, screen-time-based assignment, the dual-task session, the personalised feed interruption, the surveys, the experimenter-run procedure — was never attempted or simulated; `instrument.json` records why each of its six components is unservable in a browser.
